How We Work

Common Questions

What should clients know about your pricing?

We offer transparent pricing based on the size and condition of your space. We provide flat-rate options for standard services and customized quotes for specialized projects. Recurring clients also benefit from loyalty discounts for scheduled maintenance.

What is your typical process for working with a new client?

The process begins with a brief consultation to understand your specific needs. Once scheduled, our professional team arrives on time, follows a comprehensive cleaning checklist, and performs a final walkthrough to ensure results meet our high standards and your complete satisfaction.

What types of clients do you work with?

We serve a diverse range of clients, including homeowners, property managers, and business owners. Our portfolio covers everything from luxury residential estates and corporate offices to specialized post-construction cleaning projects.
